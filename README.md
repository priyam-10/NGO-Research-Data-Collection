## 📌 Overview

This project was completed as **Task 1 of my internship with InAmigos Foundation**.

The task focused on researching and collecting information about **10 NGOs working in India** across different social sectors, including education, animal welfare, women empowerment, healthcare, and environmental conservation.

The collected information was organized into a structured Excel spreadsheet to make the data clear, accessible, and easy to compare.

---

## 🎯 Task Objectives

The main objectives of this task were:

- 🔍 Research **10 NGOs working in India**
- 🌐 Identify and record their **official websites**
- 🎯 Understand their **primary areas of work**
- 📍 Identify their **headquarters**
- ✨ Document their **key initiatives**
- 📊 Organize the collected information in a structured format
- 🧠 Develop practical **research and data collection skills**
- 📑 Improve documentation and information organization

---

## 📋 Information Collected

For each NGO, the following information was researched and documented:

| Category | Information |
|:--|:--|
| 🏢 **NGO Name** | Name of the organization |
| 🌐 **Official Website** | Official website of the NGO |
| 🎯 **Area of Work** | Primary social sector or focus area |
| 📍 **Headquarters** | Location of the organization's headquarters |
| ✨ **Key Initiatives** | Major programs and initiatives |

## 🔍 Research Approach

The project followed a structured research and data-collection process:

```text
                 NGO Research Task
                        │
                        ▼
              Identify 10 NGOs in India
                        │
                        ▼
              Research Reliable Sources
                        │
                        ▼
              Verify Organizational Data
                        │
                        ▼
        Collect Key Information for Each NGO
                        │
                        ▼
          Organize Data in Excel
                        │
                        ▼
             Review & Documentation
```

The research focused on collecting relevant organizational information from reliable online sources, with emphasis on official NGO information.
